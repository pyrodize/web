Anatomy of Money Bot

Anatomy of Money Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Anatomy of Money YouTube channel to manage that channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload the channel's own videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • read comments on the channel's videos and post replies written or approved by the channel owner;
  • read the channel's own video statistics and analytics.

It is not offered to the public and accesses no other accounts or channels.
