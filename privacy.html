Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Anatomy of Money Bot ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Anatomy of Money YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses that channel's YouTube data: its videos and their metadata, comments on its videos (including commenters' public display names and comment text), and its analytics.

How the data is used

Only to upload and manage the channel's own videos, to read and reply to comments on them, and to review the channel's performance. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

The app does not keep a database. Data is processed while a task runs and is not stored afterwards, except the owner's OAuth credentials, which are kept privately by the owner.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the Anatomy of Money channel owner through the channel's YouTube page.
